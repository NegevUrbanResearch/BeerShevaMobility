# Beer Sheva Mobility Study — Dataset Details

Extracted from `dataset-details.html`. Captures the qualitative methodology that is not present in the CSV exports.

## Primary Data Source

- High-precision smartphone GPS data (meter-level accuracy).
- Aggregated by Decell and preprocessed by Adalya.
- Bidirectional Origin-Destination data between statistical zones and POIs.
- Fully anonymized and privacy-protected.

## Spatial Analysis Units

- CBS statistical areas for residential zones.
- 13 key Points of Interest in the Beer Sheva metro.

## Temporal Coverage

- Stage 1: November 2019 - February 2020.
- Stage 2: July 2021 (post-COVID comparison).
- Average weekday (excluding Sunday and Thursday) mobility patterns.

## Key Data Features

- Transportation modes (Car, Bus, Train, Walk, Bike).
- Trip purpose classification (Work vs. Other).
- Hourly temporal resolution.

## Data Quality

This dataset captures the majority of trips within the district. Sample balancing was applied
during preprocessing to ensure demographic representativity, though some inherent biases may
remain. All data has undergone rigorous validation by Decell and Adalya.

## Innovation District Destinations

The Innovation District total is the sum of inbound trips to its three anchor POIs:
Ben-Gurion University (BGU), Soroka Medical Center, and the Gav-Yam High-Tech Park.
